Qatar Distribution Company for Technology(QDIST)

Qatar Distribution Company for Technology (QDIST) is the largest stocker and wholesale technology distributor in Qatar. QDIST creates sales and profitability opportunities for vendors and resellers through unique marketing programs, dedicated pre- and post-sales support, financial services, and reliable product aggregation and distribution.
